PROCUREMENT POLICY MANUAL (SAMPLE)

Section 1: Purchase Approval Thresholds
All purchases below $5,000 may be approved by a Department Manager.
Purchases between $5,000 and $50,000 require approval from the Director
of Procurement. Purchases exceeding $50,000 require a formal competitive
bidding process with a minimum of three vendor quotes and sign-off from
the Chief Financial Officer.

Section 2: Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must complete a Vendor Registration Form and pass a
compliance check, including a valid tax identification number, proof of
insurance, and a signed Code of Conduct agreement. Onboarding typically
takes 5-7 business days once all documents are submitted.

Section 3: Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date unless otherwise
negotiated in the master service agreement. Early payment discounts of
2% Net 10 may be offered at the discretion of the Procurement team.

Section 4: Contract Renewal
Contracts are reviewed 90 days before expiration. Auto-renewal clauses
require written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the
contract end date, unless the contract states otherwise.

Section 5: Emergency Procurement
In cases of urgent operational need, an Emergency Purchase Order may be
issued without the standard three-quote requirement, provided it is
retroactively approved by the Director of Procurement within 5 business
days and documented with a justification memo.
