SUPPLIER ONBOARDING AND QUALITY MANUAL
Version 2.1 - Global Procurement Office

Section 1: Supplier Onboarding
Every new supplier must submit the following documents before the first purchase order is issued: GST registration certificate, PAN card copy, cancelled cheque with bank details, and a signed Supplier Code of Conduct. Onboarding is completed within 10 business days after all documents are received. Suppliers are not allowed to ship goods until the onboarding status shows Approved.

Section 2: Quality Requirements
Suppliers of physical goods must hold a valid ISO 9001 certification and share the certificate every year. The maximum accepted defect rate is 2% per delivery lot. Rejected goods must be replaced within 7 days at the supplier's cost, including the return freight.

Section 3: Delivery and Packaging
The on-time delivery target for all suppliers is 95% measured monthly. A delivery is on time if it arrives within 2 days of the confirmed date. Every shipment must include a packing slip that shows the purchase order number, item codes and quantities. Shipments without a packing slip may be refused at the warehouse gate.

Section 4: Invoicing
Each invoice must quote the purchase order number and must be submitted within 30 days of delivery. Invoices without a PO number are returned unpaid and must be corrected and resubmitted. Duplicate invoices are rejected automatically by the accounts payable system.

Section 5: Audits and Corrective Action
The company may audit a supplier's facilities with 14 days' written notice. After an audit finding, the supplier must submit a corrective action plan within 10 business days. Repeated unresolved findings can lead to suspension from the approved supplier list.
